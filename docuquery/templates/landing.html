PRIVATE BY DESIGN

Your papers stay on your machine

⌂

On-device AI

A small Qwen model runs through llama.cpp on your own CPU or GPU. Answers are generated locally — nothing is sent to an external AI service.

▣

Local storage only

Accounts, summaries, embeddings and the uploaded PDFs live in the instance/ folder inside the project. No cloud database, no storage bucket, no external sync.

⇄

Loopback network

The app and the model server listen on 127.0.0.1 only. Your Wi-Fi network and the internet cannot reach them.

✂

Nothing to track you

No analytics, no telemetry, no third-party CDN and no external fonts — the interface ships its own CSS and icons.

First setup downloads the model once (~1.1 GB). After that you can unplug the internet and everything keeps working.

HOW IT WORKS

From PDF to grounded answer

Four steps, all on your laptop — the paper never leaves it.

  1. 1

    Upload

    Validate the PDF and extract its text page by page.

    Opens your file locally and reads only its text.

  2. 2

    Index

    Detect sections, split passages, compute embeddings.

    Turns the paper into a searchable local index.

  3. 3

    Retrieve

    Pick the few passages that actually answer you.

    Looks up the right paragraphs before writing.

  4. 4

    Answer

    Write the brief from those passages, with page links.

    Every claim shows the page it came from.

IN PLAIN WORDS

What the promises on this page mean

⌂“100% on-device” — the AI is a file on your laptop

The answering model is a ~1 GB file that runs on your CPU or GPU, like a normal app. It is not a website and needs no login.

▣“No data leaves your laptop” — nothing is uploaded

Uploading a PDF means “read it into this app”, not “send it to a server”. Your paper, summaries and account stay in the project folder.

⌕“Grounded with citations” — you can check every line

Answers point to pages like [p. 4]. Click the page chip and the original PDF opens at that page, so you never have to trust blindly.

⇄“Works offline” — internet only for the first setup

The model is downloaded once. After that you can switch off Wi-Fi and still upload, summarise and ask questions.

✦“Small model” — fits a normal laptop, with limits

A 0.5B–3B model runs in 4–8 GB of RAM. It can misread numbers, which is exactly why every answer carries its page.

◎“Local accounts” — this is a single-laptop tool

Email and password are stored in a local database on your machine. It is built for one person on one laptop, not for the internet.
